MedNeXt-Lite achieves higher mean Dice than nnU-Net on all three tasks, with reported p-values of 0.002 for cardiac MRI, 0.018 for lung CT, and 0.48 for brain MRI (Table 3). Left-ventricle Dice on cardiac MRI rises from 85.6% to 89.4% (+3.8 points, 95% bootstrap confidence interval [CI] 2.4 to 5.2). Lesion Dice on lung CT rises from 84.3% to 87.2% (+2.9 points, 95% CI 1.1 to 4.7). On brain MRI, the estimated gain on glioma core is 0.4 points (95% CI -0.8 to 1.6). That interval includes both negative and positive differences, so these results do not establish equivalence. Mean Dice for all three networks on this task falls between 87.7% and 88.1%. SwinUNETR's mean Dice is within 0.6 points of nnU-Net's on every task. MedNeXt-Lite exceeds SwinUNETR by 3.2 points on cardiac MRI and 3.3 on lung CT. Table 3 reports no direct test against SwinUNETR, so we make no significance claim for those gaps. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these scores with 4.1M parameters versus 31M for nnU-Net, an 87% reduction. Parameter count alone does not establish lower runtime or memory use.
