MedNeXt-Lite achieves statistically supported Dice gains over nnU-Net on cardiac MRI and lung CT, while the brain MRI comparison is inconclusive (Table 3). The gain is largest for left-ventricle segmentation (+3.8 points) and smaller for lung lesions (+2.9 points), and the 95% bootstrap confidence intervals for both exclude zero. For glioma-core segmentation, the estimated gain is 0.4 points, and the confidence interval includes both a modest loss and a modest improvement. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net on every task. MedNeXt-Lite scores about 3 points higher than SwinUNETR on cardiac MRI and lung CT. Table 3 reports no direct test against SwinUNETR, so we make no significance claim for those gaps. The cardiac and lung gains come with about 87% fewer parameters than nnU-Net (4.1M versus 31M).
