Table 6 reports test accuracy for no augmentation, SMOTE, and TabMix on four datasets. TabMix gains most on Churn-small (2,000 rows), improving accuracy by 5.3 points over no augmentation and 3.7 points over SMOTE. Both gaps are several times the run-to-run standard deviation (0.9 to 1.1). Adult and Credit-default show differences of at most 0.5 points among the three methods, comparable to the standard deviations (0.2 to 0.5), so no method is clearly ahead. On Covertype, both augmentation methods reduce accuracy relative to no augmentation, by 0.5 points for SMOTE and 0.2 for TabMix. The gain on Churn-small, a single dataset, does not establish that TabMix improves accuracy across low-data settings.
