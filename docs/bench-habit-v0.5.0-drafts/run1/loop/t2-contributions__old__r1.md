This paper contributes FlashKV, a runtime that pages and compresses the KV cache for long-context LLM serving, together with an evaluation against vLLM. Table 2 reports results on Llama-3-70B using one 8xH100 node at context lengths from 4k to 64k. At 4k, 16k, and 32k, FlashKV reduces KV-cache memory relative to vLLM by 55%, 59%, and 63%, respectively. Over the same lengths, the decoding speedup over vLLM grows with context length, from 1.10x at 4k to 1.48x at 16k and 1.87x at 32k. At 64k, FlashKV completes decoding, while vLLM runs out of memory.
