Table 6 reports test accuracy for no augmentation, SMOTE, and TabMix on four datasets. TabMix gains most on Churn-small (2,000 rows), improving accuracy by 5.3 points over no augmentation and 3.7 points over SMOTE. Both gaps are several times the run-to-run standard deviation (0.9 to 1.1). Adult shows differences of at most 0.2 points among the three methods, comparable to the standard deviations (0.2 to 0.3). On Credit-default, SMOTE and TabMix each gain 0.4 to 0.5 points over no augmentation, a small gain comparable to the standard deviations (0.4 to 0.5). On Covertype, both augmentation methods reduce accuracy relative to no augmentation, by 0.5 points for SMOTE and 0.2 for TabMix. Because Churn-small is the only small dataset we tested, we cannot say whether its gain extends to other low-data settings.
