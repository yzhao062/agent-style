MedNeXt-Lite improves Dice over nnU-Net by 3.8 points on cardiac MRI and 2.9 points on lung CT (Table 3). The 95% bootstrap confidence intervals for both differences exclude zero. On brain MRI, the 0.4-point difference is inconclusive: its interval spans a modest loss and a modest gain. MedNeXt-Lite also has higher mean Dice than SwinUNETR on cardiac MRI and lung CT, and all three models fall within 0.4 points of each other on brain MRI. Table 3 lacks a direct test against SwinUNETR, so these comparisons are descriptive. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these scores with about 87% fewer parameters than nnU-Net (4.1M versus 31M).
