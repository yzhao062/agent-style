On Adult and Credit-default (Table 6), the three methods fall within 0.5 points of each other, about the size of the run-to-run standard deviation. SMOTE leads TabMix by 0.1 points on both. Covertype is the one dataset where augmentation lowers accuracy: TabMix scores 0.2 points and SMOTE 0.5 points below no augmentation (92.6%). TabMix shows a clear gain on Churn-small, the smallest dataset at 2,000 rows. There it reaches 81.7% accuracy, 5.3 points above no augmentation and 3.7 points above SMOTE. Both gaps exceed three standard deviations, which range from 0.9 to 1.1 points on this dataset. With one small dataset in the suite, this pattern suggests that TabMix helps most when training data are scarce.
