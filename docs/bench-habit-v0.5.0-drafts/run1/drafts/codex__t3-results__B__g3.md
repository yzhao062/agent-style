MedNeXt-Lite improved Dice over nnU-Net for left ventricle and lung lesion segmentation by 3.8 and 2.9 percentage points, respectively, with both confidence intervals excluding zero (Table 3). For glioma core segmentation, the estimated gain was small and statistically inconclusive, with the confidence interval spanning zero. MedNeXt-Lite also achieved higher Dice point estimates than SwinUNETR across all three tasks; statistical comparisons with SwinUNETR were unavailable. With 4.1M parameters versus nnU-Net’s 31M, MedNeXt-Lite used approximately 87% fewer parameters while improving segmentation accuracy for two of the three targets.
