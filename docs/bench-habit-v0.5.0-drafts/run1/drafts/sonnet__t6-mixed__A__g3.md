Table 6 reports test accuracy as mean ± std over 10 runs. On the three large datasets, TabMix and SMOTE stay within 0.5 points of no augmentation. Adult shows the smallest spread (85.1% for no augmentation, 85.3% for SMOTE, 85.2% for TabMix), and both methods gain 0.4 to 0.5 points on Credit-default. SMOTE leads TabMix by 0.1 points on both datasets, inside the standard deviations of 0.3 to 0.5. Covertype is the exception: augmentation lowers accuracy from 92.6% to 92.1% with SMOTE and to 92.4% with TabMix. The SMOTE drop exceeds the standard deviations of 0.1 to 0.2 and likely reflects a real loss. Churn-small (2,000 rows) shows the largest effect. TabMix reaches 81.7%, which is 5.3 points above no augmentation (76.4%) and 3.7 points above SMOTE (78.0%). SMOTE itself gains 1.6 points. The TabMix margin over SMOTE exceeds three standard deviations (0.9 to 1.0 points per method). Churn-small is the only small dataset we tested, so the small-data benefit rests on one dataset, and we ran no significance tests.
