On cardiac MRI and lung CT, MedNeXt-Lite improves Dice over nnU-Net by 3.8 and 2.9 points, and the 95% bootstrap intervals for both differences exclude zero (Table 3). SwinUNETR shows no comparable gain: it scores 0.6 points above nnU-Net on cardiac MRI and 0.4 points below it on lung CT. Brain MRI (glioma core) gives a different result. The three models score within 0.4 points of one another, and the 0.4-point difference between MedNeXt-Lite and nnU-Net has an interval that spans zero, so we detect no significant difference. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these scores with 4.1M parameters, 7.6 times fewer than the 31M in nnU-Net.
