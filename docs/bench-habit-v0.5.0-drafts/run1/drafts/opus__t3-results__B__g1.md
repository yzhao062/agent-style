With 4.1M parameters, 13% of nnU-Net's 31M, MedNeXt-Lite improves Dice over nnU-Net on two of the three tasks and is statistically indistinguishable from it on the third (Table 3). The gains are 3.8 points on cardiac MRI (left ventricle) and 2.9 points on lung CT lesions, and both bootstrap confidence intervals exclude zero. On brain MRI, all three networks segment the glioma core within 0.4 points of one another. MedNeXt-Lite's difference from nnU-Net there is not significant (p = 0.48), and the confidence interval limits any deficit to 0.8 points. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net on every task. Although we tested significance only against nnU-Net, MedNeXt-Lite leads SwinUNETR by similar mean margins: 3.2 points on cardiac MRI and 3.3 points on lung CT.
