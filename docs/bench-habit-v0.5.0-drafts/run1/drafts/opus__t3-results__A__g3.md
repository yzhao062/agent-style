Table 3 compares MedNeXt-Lite with nnU-Net and SwinUNETR on the three segmentation tasks. On cardiac MRI, MedNeXt-Lite segments the left ventricle at 89.4% Dice, 3.8 points above nnU-Net (95% CI [2.4, 5.2]; p = 0.002). The gain on lung CT lesions is smaller, 2.9 points (95% CI [1.1, 4.7]; p = 0.018), and remains significant under Holm correction for the three comparisons. For glioma core segmentation on brain MRI, the difference shrinks to 0.4 points, and its confidence interval spans zero (95% CI [-0.8, 1.6]; p = 0.48). On this task, all three methods fall within 0.4 points of one another, so the data show no detectable advantage for any of them. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net on every task and gives no consistent improvement over the convolutional baseline. MedNeXt-Lite thus exceeds nnU-Net on two of three organs and performs comparably on the third, while using 4.1M parameters, about 13% of nnU-Net's 31M.
