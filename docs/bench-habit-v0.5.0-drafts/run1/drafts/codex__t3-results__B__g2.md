MedNeXt-Lite improved Dice over nnU-Net for left-ventricle and lung-lesion segmentation by 3.8 and 2.9 percentage points, respectively, with both confidence intervals excluding zero (Table 3). For glioma-core segmentation, the estimated gain was small and statistically uncertain, with the confidence interval spanning zero. Mean Dice also exceeded SwinUNETR across all three tasks, although the reported significance tests compare MedNeXt-Lite with nnU-Net. MedNeXt-Lite achieved these results with 4.1 million parameters versus nnU-Net’s 31 million, an 87% reduction.
