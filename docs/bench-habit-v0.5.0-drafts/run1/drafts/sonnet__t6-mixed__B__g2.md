On Adult and Credit-default, SMOTE and TabMix land within 0.5 points of no augmentation, roughly one run-to-run standard deviation (Table 6). The two methods differ from each other by 0.1 points. Both lower accuracy on Covertype, SMOTE by 0.5 points and TabMix by 0.2. Churn-small, the only low-data dataset we tested (2,000 rows), shows the one large effect: TabMix reaches 81.7%, which is 5.3 points above no augmentation and 3.7 points above SMOTE. Both gaps exceed three standard deviations.
