MedNeXt-Lite improves Dice over nnU-Net on two of the three tasks (Table 3). The gain is 3.8 points for the left ventricle in cardiac MRI and 2.9 points for lung lesions in CT, both significant at p < 0.02. On glioma core in brain MRI, the difference shrinks to 0.4 points and is not significant (p = 0.48); its confidence interval excludes a deficit larger than 0.8 points. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net on every task. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these scores with 4.1M parameters, about 13% of nnU-Net's 31M.
