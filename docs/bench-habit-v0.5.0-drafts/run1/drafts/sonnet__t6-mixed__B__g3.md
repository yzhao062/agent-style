Table 6 reports accuracy for the three methods on four datasets. On Adult, Credit-default, and Covertype, the methods differ by 0.5 points or less. TabMix trails SMOTE by 0.1 points on Adult and Credit-default and leads it by 0.3 points on Covertype. On Credit-default, SMOTE and TabMix raise accuracy over no augmentation by 0.5 and 0.4 points, about one standard deviation. Covertype accuracy falls under both augmentations, by 0.5 points for SMOTE and 0.2 for TabMix. The largest differences appear on the 2,000-row Churn-small. SMOTE gains 1.6 points over no augmentation, and TabMix gains 5.3 points, reaching 81.7%. TabMix leads SMOTE there by 3.7 points, more than three standard deviations of any method on that dataset.
