TabMix stays within one standard deviation of the best-scoring method on the three larger datasets (Table 6). On Adult and Credit-default, both augmentation methods add at most 0.5 points over no augmentation, and SMOTE leads TabMix by 0.1 points on each. Augmentation slightly lowers accuracy on Covertype, where TabMix loses 0.2 points relative to no augmentation and SMOTE loses 0.5. TabMix separates from the baselines only on Churn-small, which has 2,000 rows. There it reaches 81.7%, which is 5.3 points above no augmentation and 3.7 points above SMOTE. Both gaps exceed three times the standard deviation of any method. Churn-small is the only small dataset in the benchmark, so these results suggest that TabMix helps most when training data are scarce.
