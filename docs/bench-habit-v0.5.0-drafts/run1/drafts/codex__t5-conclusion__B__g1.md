## Conclusion

AdaptRL learns residual dynamics online to transfer grasping policies from simulation to a real robot. Across five object categories, it achieves higher grasp success than domain randomization and DAgger in 100 real-robot trials per category (Table 5). Improvements over DAgger range from 11 to 24 percentage points, with the largest gains on reflective and transparent objects. These results support online residual dynamics learning as an effective approach to simulation-to-real transfer for grasping across the tested object categories.
