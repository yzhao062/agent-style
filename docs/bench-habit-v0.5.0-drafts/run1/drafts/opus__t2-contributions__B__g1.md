This paper makes two contributions. First, we design and build FlashKV, a runtime for long-context LLM serving that pages the KV cache and compresses it to shrink its memory footprint. Second, we evaluate FlashKV against vLLM with Llama-3-70B on a single node of eight H100 GPUs. Across context lengths from 4k to 64k tokens, FlashKV reduces KV-cache memory by 55% to 63% (Table 2). Its decoding speedup over vLLM grows with context length, from 1.10× at 4k tokens to 1.87× at 32k tokens. At 64k tokens, vLLM runs out of memory on this node, while FlashKV continues to serve requests.
