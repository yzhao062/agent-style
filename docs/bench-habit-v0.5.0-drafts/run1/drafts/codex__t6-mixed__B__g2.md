TabMix’s accuracy gains are concentrated on Churn-small, the dataset with 2,000 rows (Table 6). It improves mean accuracy by 5.3 percentage points over no augmentation and 3.7 points over SMOTE, while also reducing variability across runs. On Adult, Credit-default, and Covertype, TabMix performs similarly to both baselines, with mean accuracy differences of at most 0.4 percentage points.
