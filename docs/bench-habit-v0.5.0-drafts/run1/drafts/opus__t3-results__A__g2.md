Table 3 reports Dice scores with 95% bootstrap confidence intervals (CIs) for the three tasks. On cardiac MRI, MedNeXt-Lite segments the left ventricle at 89.4% Dice, 3.8 points above nnU-Net (95% CI 2.4 to 5.2; p = 0.002). The gain on lung CT lesions is smaller, at 2.9 points (95% CI 1.1 to 4.7; p = 0.018). This p-value sits just above the Bonferroni threshold for three comparisons (0.05/3 ≈ 0.017), so we treat the lung result as suggestive. On glioma core, all three models fall within 0.4 points of one another (MedNeXt-Lite vs nnU-Net: +0.4 points; 95% CI -0.8 to 1.6; p = 0.48). That interval rules out a MedNeXt-Lite deficit larger than 0.8 points. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net on every task. MedNeXt-Lite outscores SwinUNETR by 3.2, 3.3, and 0.2 points on cardiac, lung, and brain, though Table 3 includes no paired test for this comparison. With 4.1M parameters to nnU-Net's 31M, MedNeXt-Lite showed no measured loss in Dice on any of the three tasks.
