MedNeXt-Lite improves Dice over nnU-Net for left-ventricle and lung-lesion segmentation by 3.8 and 2.9 percentage points, respectively (Table 3). Both difference confidence intervals exclude zero, supporting an improvement on these tasks. For glioma-core segmentation, the estimated gain is small and its confidence interval includes zero, providing no clear evidence of improvement over nnU-Net. MedNeXt-Lite also achieves higher mean Dice than SwinUNETR across all three tasks. These results show task-dependent segmentation gains with 4.1 million parameters, compared with 31 million for nnU-Net.
