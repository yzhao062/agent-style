MedNeXt-Lite significantly outperforms nnU-Net on two of the three tasks (Table 3). Dice rises by 3.8 points for the left ventricle on cardiac MRI and by 2.9 points for lesions on lung CT. For glioma core on brain MRI, the gain shrinks to a non-significant 0.4 points, and the three models span 87.7 to 88.1 Dice. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net throughout and trails MedNeXt-Lite's point estimate on all three tasks, though Table 3 tests significance against nnU-Net alone. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these scores with 4.1M parameters against 31M for nnU-Net, a 7.6-fold reduction.
