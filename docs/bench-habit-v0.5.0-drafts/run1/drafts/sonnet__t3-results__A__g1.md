MedNeXt-Lite improves Dice over nnU-Net on two of the three tasks (Table 3). Left-ventricle Dice on cardiac MRI rises from 85.6% to 89.4% (+3.8 points, 95% CI 2.4 to 5.2, p = 0.002). Lesion Dice on lung CT rises from 84.3% to 87.2% (+2.9 points, 95% CI 1.1 to 4.7, p = 0.018). On brain MRI, the 0.4-point gain on glioma core (95% CI -0.8 to 1.6, p = 0.48) is indistinguishable from zero. The interval also allows a deficit of up to 0.8 points, so the data cannot separate the two models, and all three networks score between 87.7% and 88.1% on this task. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net on every task, while MedNeXt-Lite exceeds it by 3.2 points on cardiac MRI and 3.3 points on lung CT. Table 3 reports no direct test against SwinUNETR, so we make no significance claim for those gaps. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these scores with 4.1M parameters, 7.6 times fewer than the 31M of nnU-Net.
