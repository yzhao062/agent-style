On cardiac MRI and lung CT, MedNeXt-Lite raises Dice over nnU-Net by 3.8 points (p = 0.002) and 2.9 points (p = 0.018), and both 95% bootstrap intervals exclude zero (Table 3). SwinUNETR differs from nnU-Net by at most 0.6 points on every task, so it offers no comparable gain. On glioma-core segmentation in brain MRI, the three methods score within 0.4 points of one another, and the MedNeXt-Lite difference is not significant (p = 0.48). The interval for that difference, [-0.8, 1.6], rules out a deficit larger than 0.8 points. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these results with 4.1M parameters against 31M for nnU-Net, a 7.6-fold reduction.
