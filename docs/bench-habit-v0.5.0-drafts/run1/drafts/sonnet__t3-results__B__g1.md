MedNeXt-Lite improves Dice over nnU-Net on two of the three tasks (Table 3). On cardiac MRI it gains 3.8 Dice points on the left ventricle (95% CI 2.4 to 5.2, p = 0.002), and on lung CT it gains 2.9 points on lesions (95% CI 1.1 to 4.7, p = 0.018). Both intervals exclude zero. On brain MRI, the 0.4-point gain on the glioma core has an interval spanning zero (-0.8 to 1.6, p = 0.48), so the data show no significant difference from nnU-Net. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net on every task, while MedNeXt-Lite exceeds it by 3.2 points on cardiac MRI and 3.3 on lung CT. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these scores with 4.1M parameters, about one-eighth of the 31M in nnU-Net.
