We introduce FlashKV, a runtime that pages and compresses the key-value (KV) cache for long-context large language model serving. Our evaluation of Llama-3-70B on one node with eight H100 GPUs shows 55% to 63% lower KV-cache memory use than vLLM (Table 2). FlashKV accelerates decoding by 1.10x to 1.87x at contexts of 4k to 32k tokens and serves 64k-token contexts, where vLLM runs out of memory.
