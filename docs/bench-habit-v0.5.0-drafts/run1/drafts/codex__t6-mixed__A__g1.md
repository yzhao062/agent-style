Table 6 reports classification accuracy as mean ± standard deviation over 10 runs. TabMix achieves its largest gain on Churn-small (2,000 rows), reaching 81.7 ± 0.9%, exceeding no augmentation by 5.3 percentage points and SMOTE by 3.7 points. On Adult and Credit-default, TabMix improves over no augmentation by 0.1 and 0.4 percentage points, respectively, while trailing SMOTE by 0.1 point on both datasets. On Covertype, TabMix reaches 92.4 ± 0.2%, falling 0.2 percentage points below no augmentation and exceeding SMOTE by 0.3 points.
