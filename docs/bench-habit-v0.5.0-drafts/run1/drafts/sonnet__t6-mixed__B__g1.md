Table 6 compares no augmentation, SMOTE, and TabMix on four datasets. On Adult, all three methods fall within 0.2 points of each other. On Credit-default, SMOTE and TabMix gain 0.5 and 0.4 points over no augmentation, about one standard deviation. Covertype is the exception: both augmentation methods lose accuracy, SMOTE by 0.5 points and TabMix by 0.2. The clear gain appears on Churn-small (2,000 rows), where SMOTE improves on no augmentation by 1.6 points and TabMix by 5.3 points, several times the run-to-run standard deviation of about 1 point.
