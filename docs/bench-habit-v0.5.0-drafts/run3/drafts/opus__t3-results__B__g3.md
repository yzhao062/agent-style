MedNeXt-Lite improves Dice over nnU-Net on two of the three tasks (Table 3): by 3.8 points for the left ventricle in cardiac MRI (p = 0.002) and by 2.9 points for lesions in lung CT (p = 0.018). Both gains remain significant after Holm-Bonferroni correction for the three comparisons. On glioma core in brain MRI, the data cannot separate the two networks: the 0.4-point difference has a 95% CI of [-0.8, 1.6] and p = 0.48. SwinUNETR differs from nnU-Net by at most 0.6 points on any task. Its point estimates trail MedNeXt-Lite's by 3.2 points on cardiac MRI and 3.3 points on lung CT. We tested significance only against nnU-Net, so these gaps remain untested. No task shows a measurable Dice loss for MedNeXt-Lite, which uses 4.1M parameters, about one-eighth of nnU-Net's 31M.
