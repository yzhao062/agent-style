Against nnU-Net, MedNeXt-Lite raises Dice by 3.8 points on cardiac MRI (95% bootstrap confidence interval [CI] 2.4 to 5.2, p = 0.002). On lung CT the gain is 2.9 points (CI 1.1 to 4.7, p = 0.018; Table 3). The lung gain is the weaker of the two: its interval lower bound is 1.1 points, and p = 0.018 exceeds the Bonferroni threshold of 0.017 for three organs. On brain MRI, the 0.4-point difference is within noise (CI -0.8 to 1.6, p = 0.48), so we detect no difference. The interval excludes a loss beyond 0.8 points. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net on every organ, and Table 3 reports no paired test between SwinUNETR and MedNeXt-Lite. Its point estimates trail MedNeXt-Lite by 3.2 points on cardiac MRI and 3.3 points on lung CT. All three results come from a model with 4.1M parameters, 7.6 times fewer than the 31M in nnU-Net.
