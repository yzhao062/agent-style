MedNeXt-Lite improved Dice over nnU-Net by 3.8 percentage points for left-ventricular segmentation and 2.9 points for lung lesion segmentation, with both confidence intervals excluding zero (Table 3). For glioma core segmentation, the difference was small and statistically inconclusive (0.4 points; 95% CI, -0.8 to 1.6; p = 0.48). MedNeXt-Lite also achieved higher Dice point estimates than SwinUNETR across all three tasks, although Table 3 provides no statistical comparisons against SwinUNETR. The cardiac and lung gains accompany approximately 87% fewer parameters than nnU-Net (4.1M versus 31M), supporting parameter-efficient segmentation for these tasks.
