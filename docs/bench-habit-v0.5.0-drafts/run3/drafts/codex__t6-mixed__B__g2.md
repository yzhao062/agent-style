Table 6 shows that TabMix improves accuracy most on Churn-small (2,000 rows), gaining 5.3 percentage points over no augmentation and 3.7 points over SMOTE. On Adult and Credit-default, TabMix yields small gains over no augmentation and trails SMOTE by 0.1 points; these differences are comparable to the reported standard deviations. On Covertype, TabMix trails no augmentation by 0.2 points and exceeds SMOTE by 0.3 points. The results support TabMix’s benefit on Churn-small, while showing no consistent advantage across the other three datasets.
