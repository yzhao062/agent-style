Table 7 shows that TrafficCast reduces 60-minute forecast mean absolute error relative to GraphWaveNet by 0.41 km/h on Metro-A and 0.35 km/h on Metro-B. On Coastal, the reduction is 0.02 km/h, smaller than the reported standard deviations across seeds; on Rural-Net, error increases by 0.19 km/h. TrafficCast also takes 41 ms per batch versus 29 ms for GraphWaveNet, a 41% increase in inference time. These results support an accuracy advantage on the two largest evaluated networks, with higher inference cost and uneven performance across smaller networks.
