MedNeXt-Lite improves Dice over nnU-Net on cardiac MRI and lung CT. On brain MRI, the two networks show no significant difference (Table 3). The largest gain is on the left ventricle in cardiac MRI, where MedNeXt-Lite reaches 89.4% Dice against 85.6% for nnU-Net (+3.8 points, 95% CI [2.4, 5.2], p = 0.002). On lung CT lesions, MedNeXt-Lite gains 2.9 points (95% CI [1.1, 4.7], p = 0.018). That interval excludes zero, but the p-value narrowly misses the Bonferroni-corrected threshold of 0.017 for three tasks, so the lung result is weaker evidence than the cardiac one. For glioma core segmentation, all three networks fall within 0.4 points of one another, and the difference from nnU-Net is within noise (+0.4, 95% CI [−0.8, 1.6], p = 0.48). SwinUNETR, the transformer baseline, stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net on every task, and their confidence intervals overlap. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these results with 4.1M parameters, 13% of nnU-Net's 31M.
