TabMix improves accuracy clearly on one of the four datasets, the 2,000-row Churn-small (Table 6). It reaches 81.7%, which is 5.3 points above no augmentation (76.4%) and 3.7 points above SMOTE (78.0%). Both gaps exceed three times the largest standard deviation on that dataset (1.1). Adult shows no measurable effect: the three methods span 85.1% to 85.3%, within the run-to-run standard deviations of 0.2 to 0.3. On Credit-default, SMOTE (82.4%) and TabMix (82.3%) score 0.5 and 0.4 points above no augmentation (81.9%), about one standard deviation, and differ from each other by 0.1 points. On Covertype, no augmentation scores highest, at 92.6% versus 92.4% for TabMix and 92.1% for SMOTE. SMOTE's 0.5-point drop is 2.5 times its standard deviation, while TabMix's 0.2-point drop equals its own.
