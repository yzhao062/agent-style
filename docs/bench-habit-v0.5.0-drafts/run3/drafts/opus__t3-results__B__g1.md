MedNeXt-Lite improves Dice over nnU-Net by 3.8 points on left-ventricle segmentation in cardiac MRI and by 2.9 points on lung CT lesions (Table 3). The 95% bootstrap confidence intervals for both differences exclude zero (p = 0.002 and p = 0.018, respectively). On glioma core in brain MRI, the 0.4-point difference is within noise because its interval spans zero (-0.8 to 1.6; p = 0.48), so this task shows no measurable gain over nnU-Net. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net on all three tasks. MedNeXt-Lite obtains these results with 4.1M parameters, 13% of the 31M in nnU-Net.
