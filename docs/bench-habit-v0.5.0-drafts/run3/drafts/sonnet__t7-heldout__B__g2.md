TrafficCast's accuracy gain depends on network size (Table 7). On Metro-A and Metro-B, the two largest networks, it beats GraphWaveNet, the strongest baseline, by 0.41 and 0.35 km/h MAE (11.6% and 10.6%). Both gaps exceed the seed standard deviations of 0.05 km/h or less. Coastal (640 sensors) is a tie: the 0.02 km/h gap falls below the seed standard deviation of 0.05 to 0.06 km/h. On Rural-Net (180 sensors), TrafficCast loses to both neural baselines: its MAE is 3.21 km/h against 3.02 for GraphWaveNet and 3.05 for DCRNN. The gap to GraphWaveNet narrows as sensor count falls and reverses on the smallest network. TrafficCast also runs slower at inference, at 41 ms per batch against 29 ms for GraphWaveNet, a 41% increase.
