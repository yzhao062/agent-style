We introduce ClimaDown, a diffusion model that downscales daily precipitation from 100 km to 10 km resolution. ClimaDown generates high-resolution precipitation fields conditioned on the coarse input. Experiments cover four regions (Alpine, Monsoon, Mediterranean, Sahel) and compare ClimaDown with bias-correction spatial disaggregation (BCSD) and a U-Net. Scores use the continuous ranked probability score (CRPS, lower is better). Relative to BCSD, ClimaDown lowers CRPS by 0.49 in the Alpine region, 0.57 in the Monsoon region, and 0.31 in the Sahel, with 95% bootstrap intervals that exclude zero (Table 8). In the Mediterranean, the 0.07 reduction is within noise (interval [-0.19, 0.05]). ClimaDown's CRPS is also lower than the U-Net's in all four regions, by 0.02 to 0.26, but we report no interval for this comparison. On extremes, ClimaDown trails the U-Net: for 99th-percentile daily precipitation, it underestimates intensity by 18% on average, twice the U-Net's 9%. Training ClimaDown takes 3.1 GPU-days, versus 0.4 for the U-Net.
