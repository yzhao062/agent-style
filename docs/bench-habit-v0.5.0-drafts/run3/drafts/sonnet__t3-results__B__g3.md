As Table 3 shows, MedNeXt-Lite raises Dice over nnU-Net by 3.8 points on cardiac MRI (95% CI 2.4 to 5.2, p = 0.002) and by 2.9 points on lung CT (CI 1.1 to 4.7, p = 0.018). The lung gain is the weaker result. Its interval lower bound is 1.1 points, and its p-value exceeds the Bonferroni threshold of 0.017 for three tasks, though it passes the Holm procedure. On brain MRI, the two models tie (+0.4 points, CI -0.8 to 1.6, p = 0.48), and the interval spans a loss of 0.8 points to a gain of 1.6. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 Dice points of nnU-Net on every task. Table 3 reports no test between MedNeXt-Lite and SwinUNETR. On cardiac MRI their intervals are disjoint (88.0 to 90.8 versus 84.8 to 87.6); on lung CT they overlap slightly (85.4 to 89.0 versus 82.0 to 85.8). MedNeXt-Lite reaches these scores with 4.1M parameters against 31M for nnU-Net, a 7.6-fold reduction.
