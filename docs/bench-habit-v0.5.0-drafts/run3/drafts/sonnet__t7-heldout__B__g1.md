Table 7 shows that the advantage of TrafficCast in 60-minute mean absolute error (MAE) grows with sensor count across the four networks. On Metro-A (2,100 sensors), it beats GraphWaveNet, the strongest baseline, by 0.41 km/h (12%); on Metro-B (1,450 sensors), by 0.35 km/h (11%). Both gaps are at least eight times the seed standard deviation. Coastal (640 sensors) is a tie within noise, 2.89 ± 0.06 against 2.91 ± 0.05. Rural-Net (180 sensors) is a loss: TrafficCast reaches 3.21 km/h, 0.19 above GraphWaveNet and 0.16 above DCRNN, and the gap exceeds the seed standard deviation of every model there. TrafficCast also takes 41% longer per batch, 41 ms against 29 ms for GraphWaveNet.
