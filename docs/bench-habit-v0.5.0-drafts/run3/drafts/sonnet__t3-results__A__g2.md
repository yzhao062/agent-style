MedNeXt-Lite attains the highest Dice point estimate on all three tasks (Table 3), but the evidence for a gain over nnU-Net differs by organ. On cardiac MRI, it improves left-ventricle Dice by 3.8 points (89.4% versus 85.6%; 95% CI [2.4, 5.2], p = 0.002). The lung CT lesion gain is 2.9 points (87.2% versus 84.3%; CI [1.1, 4.7], p = 0.018), and the intervals for both differences exclude zero. On brain MRI, the glioma-core difference is 0.4 points (CI [-0.8, 1.6], p = 0.48). That interval spans zero, so the data cannot separate the two models on this task. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 Dice points of nnU-Net on every task, with overlapping intervals. Its point estimates trail MedNeXt-Lite by 3.2 points on cardiac MRI and 3.3 on lung CT. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these scores with 4.1M parameters, about 7.6 times fewer than the 31M of nnU-Net.
