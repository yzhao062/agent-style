TrafficCast achieves the lowest mean absolute error on the two metropolitan networks, improving on GraphWaveNet by approximately 11% for 60-minute forecasts (Table 7). On Coastal, its 0.02 km/h advantage is small relative to seed-to-seed variation, while on Rural-Net it increases error by 0.19 km/h. These results show that TrafficCast’s forecasting gains depend on the network, with the clearest benefits on the metropolitan datasets. Those gains also carry a computational cost: inference takes 41 ms per batch, compared with 29 ms for GraphWaveNet.
