Table 6 reports test accuracy for no augmentation, SMOTE, and TabMix on four datasets. TabMix gains most on Churn-small, the only small dataset (2,000 rows). It reaches 81.7%, which is 5.3 points above no augmentation (76.4%) and 3.7 points above SMOTE (78.0%). Both gaps exceed the standard deviations of 0.9 to 1.1 points. On the three larger datasets, TabMix stays within about one standard deviation of no augmentation. Adult spans only 0.2 points across all three methods. Credit-default puts SMOTE (82.4%) and TabMix (82.3%) 0.5 and 0.4 points above no augmentation (81.9%), and TabMix trails SMOTE by 0.1 points. Covertype favors no augmentation (92.6%); TabMix loses 0.2 points and SMOTE loses 0.5, which leaves TabMix 0.3 points ahead of SMOTE. Because Churn-small is the only small dataset in our benchmark, we cannot say whether this gain extends to other low-data settings.
