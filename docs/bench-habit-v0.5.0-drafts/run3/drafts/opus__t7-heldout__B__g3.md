Table 7 shows that TrafficCast's advantage over the baselines shrinks as networks get smaller and reverses on the smallest one. On Metro-A and Metro-B, TrafficCast lowers 60-minute MAE relative to GraphWaveNet, the strongest baseline, by 0.41 km/h (12%) and 0.35 km/h (11%). Both gaps far exceed the seed-to-seed standard deviation, which is at most 0.05 km/h. Coastal (640 sensors) yields a tie, since the 0.02 km/h difference lies within seed noise. Rural-Net (180 sensors) reverses the ranking: TrafficCast trails GraphWaveNet by 0.19 km/h and DCRNN by 0.16 km/h. The model also runs slower, taking 41 ms per inference batch against 29 ms for GraphWaveNet. TrafficCast is thus the more accurate choice for networks of 1,450 or more sensors, provided the application can absorb the extra 12 ms per batch. On networks of 640 sensors or fewer, GraphWaveNet matches or beats it while running faster.
