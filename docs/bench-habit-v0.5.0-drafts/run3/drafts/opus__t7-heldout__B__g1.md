Table 7 shows that TrafficCast's advantage over GraphWaveNet, the strongest baseline on every network, shrinks as the sensor network gets smaller. On the two largest networks, TrafficCast's error is 12% lower on Metro-A (3.12 vs. 3.53 km/h) and 11% lower on Metro-B (2.95 vs. 3.30 km/h). Both gaps are about eight times the standard deviation across seeds. Coastal (640 sensors) is a tie: the 0.02 km/h gap is smaller than either model's seed standard deviation. On Rural-Net (180 sensors), TrafficCast has the highest MAE of the three learned models: 3.21 km/h, against 3.05 for DCRNN and 3.02 for GraphWaveNet. TrafficCast is also slower at inference, taking 41 ms per batch against 29 ms for GraphWaveNet. These results favor TrafficCast on large metropolitan networks, where the extra 12 ms per batch buys an 11 to 12% error reduction. On the two smaller networks, GraphWaveNet matches or beats TrafficCast's accuracy at lower latency.
