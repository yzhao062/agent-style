On cardiac MRI and lung CT, MedNeXt-Lite improves Dice over nnU-Net by 3.8 points (95% CI 2.4 to 5.2, p = 0.002) and 2.9 points (CI 1.1 to 4.7, p = 0.018), reaching 89.4% and 87.2% (Table 3). On brain MRI, the 0.4-point difference (CI -0.8 to 1.6, p = 0.48) is within noise. The interval admits a loss of 0.8 points or a gain of 1.6, so we treat the two networks as tied on glioma core. SwinUNETR differs from nnU-Net by +0.6, -0.4, and +0.2 points across the three tasks, and its intervals overlap those of nnU-Net on each. MedNeXt-Lite delivers its scores with 4.1M parameters, 7.6 times fewer than the 31M of nnU-Net.
