Table 7 shows that the accuracy margin of TrafficCast over GraphWaveNet, the strongest baseline, falls with network size. On Metro-A (2,100 sensors) and Metro-B (1,450 sensors), TrafficCast lowers the 60-minute mean absolute error (MAE) by 0.41 km/h (11.6%) and 0.35 km/h (10.6%). Both gaps far exceed the seed standard deviations of 0.03 to 0.05 km/h. On Coastal (640 sensors), the 0.02 km/h difference lies within seed variation, so the two models tie. On Rural-Net (180 sensors), TrafficCast loses to both learned baselines, with an MAE of 3.21 km/h against 3.02 for GraphWaveNet and 3.05 for DCRNN. The 0.19 km/h deficit to GraphWaveNet exceeds the seed standard deviations of 0.08 and 0.10 km/h. TrafficCast also costs more at inference, at 41 ms per batch against 29 ms for GraphWaveNet, a 41% increase.
