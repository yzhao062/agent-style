MedNeXt-Lite improved Dice over nnU-Net for left-ventricle and lung-lesion segmentation by 3.8 and 2.9 percentage points, respectively, with both 95% bootstrap confidence intervals excluding zero (Table 3). For glioma-core segmentation, the difference was small and statistically inconclusive (+0.4 points; 95% CI, -0.8 to 1.6; p = 0.48). Dice point estimates also exceeded those of SwinUNETR across all three tasks. With 4.1M parameters versus nnU-Net’s 31M, MedNeXt-Lite combined approximately 87% fewer parameters with improved cardiac and lung segmentation, while the brain results provide insufficient evidence of an improvement.
