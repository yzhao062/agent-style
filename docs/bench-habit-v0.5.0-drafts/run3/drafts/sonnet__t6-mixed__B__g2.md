Table 6 shows that TabMix helps clearly on one of the four datasets. On Churn-small (2,000 rows), TabMix raises accuracy from 76.4% to 81.7%, a gain of 5.3 points, roughly five times the run-to-run standard deviation of about 1 point. It also beats SMOTE by 3.7 points there. SMOTE gains 1.6 points over no augmentation on the same dataset, about 1.5 standard deviations. On Adult and Credit-default, the three methods differ by 0.5 points or less, roughly one standard deviation, so they tie: TabMix scores 85.2% against 85.3% for SMOTE on Adult, and 82.3% against 82.4% on Credit-default. On Covertype, both augmentations lower accuracy relative to the 92.6% baseline. TabMix loses 0.2 points, equal to its standard deviation, while SMOTE loses 0.5 points, more than twice its standard deviation.
