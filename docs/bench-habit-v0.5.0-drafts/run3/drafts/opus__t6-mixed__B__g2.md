Table 6 reports mean accuracy over 10 runs. TabMix gives its only clear gain on Churn-small, the one small dataset in our evaluation (2,000 rows). There it reaches 81.7%, which is 5.3 points above no augmentation and 3.7 points above SMOTE. Both gaps are more than three times the run-to-run standard deviation. Across the three larger datasets, TabMix stays within 0.3 points of SMOTE and shows no consistent gain over training without augmentation. On Adult and Credit-default, all three methods fall within 0.5 points of one another. On each of these datasets, TabMix trails SMOTE by 0.1 points, well within one standard deviation. Covertype favors no augmentation: SMOTE lowers accuracy by 0.5 points, and TabMix lowers it by 0.2 points, about one standard deviation. These results suggest that TabMix helps most when training data are scarce, but in this evaluation that pattern rests on a single dataset.
