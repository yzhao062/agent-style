Across the four networks in Table 7, TrafficCast's advantage over the strongest baseline, GraphWaveNet, shrinks as the sensor count falls. On Metro-A and Metro-B, the two largest networks, TrafficCast lowers 60-minute MAE by 0.41 km/h (12%) and 0.35 km/h (11%). Both gaps are more than eight times the largest standard deviation across seeds in their rows. On Coastal (640 sensors), the 0.02 km/h difference lies within seed variation, so the two models tie. Rural-Net (180 sensors) reverses the ranking: TrafficCast has the highest MAE of the three learned models, 0.19 km/h above GraphWaveNet. The accuracy gains also cost speed. TrafficCast takes 41 ms per batch, 12 ms (41%) more than GraphWaveNet. These results favor TrafficCast for large metropolitan networks and GraphWaveNet for small networks or deployments with tight latency limits.
