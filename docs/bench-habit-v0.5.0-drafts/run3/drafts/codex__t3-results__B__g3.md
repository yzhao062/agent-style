MedNeXt-Lite improved Dice over nnU-Net for cardiac MRI and lung CT by 3.8 and 2.9 percentage points, respectively, with both confidence intervals excluding zero (Table 3). For brain MRI, the difference was inconclusive: the estimated gain was 0.4 points, with a 95% confidence interval spanning −0.8 to 1.6 points. Mean Dice also exceeded SwinUNETR across all three tasks, although the reported statistical comparisons establish significance only against nnU-Net. MedNeXt-Lite achieved these results with 4.1M parameters compared with nnU-Net’s 31M, a reduction of approximately 87%.
