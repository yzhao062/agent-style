Table 6 reports accuracy for no augmentation, SMOTE, and TabMix on four datasets. On the three larger datasets, TabMix shows no clear gain. Its accuracy differs from no augmentation by 0.1 to 0.4 points, against standard deviations of 0.1 to 0.5. Adult shows the narrowest spread, with all three methods between 85.1% and 85.3%. On Credit-default, SMOTE (82.4%) and TabMix (82.3%) both beat no augmentation (81.9%) by about one standard deviation. Covertype favors no augmentation (92.6%): SMOTE falls 0.5 points to 92.1% and TabMix falls 0.2 points to 92.4%. The largest effect appears on Churn-small, the only small dataset (2,000 rows). TabMix reaches 81.7%, which is 5.3 points above no augmentation (76.4%) and 3.7 points above SMOTE (78.0%), gaps several times larger than the standard deviations of about 1 point.
