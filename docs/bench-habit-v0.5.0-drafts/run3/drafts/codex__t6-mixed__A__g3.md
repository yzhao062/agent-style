Table 6 shows mixed results for TabMix, with its largest gain on Churn-small. On Adult and Credit-default, TabMix improves mean accuracy over no augmentation by 0.1 and 0.4 percentage points, respectively, while trailing SMOTE by 0.1 point on both datasets. On Covertype, TabMix falls 0.2 percentage points below no augmentation and exceeds SMOTE by 0.3 points. On Churn-small (2,000 rows), TabMix reaches 81.7 ± 0.9% accuracy over 10 runs, exceeding no augmentation by 5.3 percentage points and SMOTE by 3.7 points.
