MedNeXt-Lite improves Dice over nnU-Net on two of the three organs and ties it on the third (Table 3). Cardiac MRI shows the largest gain, 3.8 points for the left ventricle (95% bootstrap CI [2.4, 5.2], p = 0.002). The lung CT lesion gain is 2.9 points with a wider interval, and its lower bound of 1.1 points still excludes zero (p = 0.018). For the glioma core in brain MRI, the 0.4-point difference lies within noise (CI [-0.8, 1.6], p = 0.48), so we detect no difference from nnU-Net. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net on every task and trails MedNeXt-Lite by 3.2 points on cardiac MRI and 3.3 on lung CT. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these scores with 4.1M parameters, 13% of the 31M in nnU-Net.
