This paper makes two contributions. First, we present FlashKV, a runtime for long-context LLM serving that pages the KV cache and compresses it so longer contexts fit in GPU memory. Second, we evaluate FlashKV against vLLM by serving Llama-3-70B on one node of eight H100 GPUs (Table 2). FlashKV reduces KV-cache memory by 55% at 4k tokens and by 63% at 32k and 64k tokens. Its decoding speedup grows with context length, from a modest 1.10x at 4k to 1.87x at 32k. At 64k tokens, vLLM runs out of memory, while FlashKV still serves the model on the same node.
