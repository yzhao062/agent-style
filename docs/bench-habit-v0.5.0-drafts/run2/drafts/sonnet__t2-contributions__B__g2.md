We present FlashKV, a runtime that pages and compresses the KV cache for long-context LLM serving. Against vLLM on Llama-3-70B with one 8xH100 node, FlashKV reduces KV-cache memory by 55% at 4k context, rising to 63% at 32k and 64k (Table 2). Its decoding speedup grows with context length, from 1.10x at 4k to 1.87x at 32k, so the 4k gain is the smallest. At 64k, vLLM runs out of memory, while FlashKV runs and keeps the 63% memory reduction.
