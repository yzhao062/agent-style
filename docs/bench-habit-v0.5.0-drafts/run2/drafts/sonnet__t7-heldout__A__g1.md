Table 7 shows that TrafficCast beats every baseline on the two largest networks, ties GraphWaveNet on Coastal, and loses to both neural baselines on Rural-Net. Against GraphWaveNet, the strongest baseline, TrafficCast cuts MAE from 3.53 to 3.12 km/h on Metro-A (11.6%) and from 3.30 to 2.95 km/h on Metro-B (10.6%). Both gaps exceed eight times the standard deviation of either method across the five seeds. Coastal is a tie: 2.89 ± 0.06 km/h versus 2.91 ± 0.05 km/h, a 0.02 km/h difference inside one standard deviation. On Rural-Net, TrafficCast's MAE of 3.21 km/h trails GraphWaveNet's 3.02 km/h by 0.19 km/h (6.3%). The margin shrinks as sensor count falls, from 0.41 km/h at 2,100 sensors to a 0.19 km/h deficit at 180. With only four networks, we cannot separate sensor count from other differences between them, so the cause of the reversal remains untested. TrafficCast also costs more at inference: 41 ms per batch versus 29 ms for GraphWaveNet, a 41% increase.
