MedNeXt-Lite improves Dice over nnU-Net by 3.8 percentage points for left-ventricular segmentation and 2.9 points for lung-lesion segmentation, with statistically significant differences on both tasks (Table 3). For glioma-core segmentation, the estimated 0.4-point increase is statistically uncertain, with a 95% confidence interval spanning zero. Dice point estimates also exceed SwinUNETR across all three tasks, although the reported tests establish significance only against nnU-Net. MedNeXt-Lite achieves these results with 4.1M parameters, approximately 87% fewer than nnU-Net’s 31M.
