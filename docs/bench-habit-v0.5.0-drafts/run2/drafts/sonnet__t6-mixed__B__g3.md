Table 6 reports accuracy as mean ± standard deviation over 10 runs. TabMix beats both baselines on one dataset only, Churn-small (2,000 rows), where it improves on no augmentation by 5.3 points and on SMOTE by 3.7 points, against standard deviations of 0.9 to 1.1. On Adult and Credit-default, the three methods differ by 0.5 points or less, comparable to the standard deviations of 0.2 to 0.5, so we treat them as tied. Covertype favors no augmentation: SMOTE loses 0.5 points, well outside the standard deviations of 0.1 and 0.2, and TabMix loses 0.2 points, within noise.
