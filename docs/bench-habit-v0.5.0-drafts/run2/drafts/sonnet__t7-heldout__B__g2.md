TrafficCast gives the lowest 60-minute MAE on the two largest networks (Table 7). It reaches 3.12 km/h on Metro-A (2,100 sensors) and 2.95 km/h on Metro-B (1,450 sensors), which is 0.41 and 0.35 km/h below GraphWaveNet, the strongest baseline. Both gaps exceed seven times the largest seed standard deviation in those rows (0.05). Coastal (640 sensors) is a tie within noise: 2.89 ± 0.06 for TrafficCast against 2.91 ± 0.05 for GraphWaveNet. TrafficCast loses on Rural-Net (180 sensors), where its 3.21 km/h MAE is 0.19 km/h above GraphWaveNet and 0.16 km/h above DCRNN, gaps larger than any model's seed standard deviation there (0.08 to 0.10). The advantage thus shrinks with sensor count and reverses on the smallest network. TrafficCast also runs slower at inference, at 41 ms per batch against 29 ms for GraphWaveNet (41% more).
