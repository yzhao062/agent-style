For 60-minute forecasts, TrafficCast reduces mean absolute error relative to GraphWaveNet by 0.41 km/h on Metro-A and 0.35 km/h on Metro-B (Table 7). On Coastal, the 0.02 km/h reduction is small relative to variation across seeds, while Rural-Net error increases by 0.19 km/h. TrafficCast also increases inference time from 29 to 41 ms per batch. These results show clear accuracy gains on the two metropolitan networks, limited benefit on Coastal, and poorer forecasts on Rural-Net, alongside higher inference cost.
