MedNeXt-Lite outperforms nnU-Net on two of the three tasks in Table 3, by 3.8 Dice points on cardiac MRI and 2.9 points on lung CT lesions. Both confidence intervals exclude zero, but the lung result (p = 0.018) misses the Bonferroni-corrected threshold of 0.0167 for three comparisons, so the cardiac gain is the stronger evidence. On brain MRI glioma core, the 0.4-point difference lies within noise, and we count this task as a tie. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net on every task. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these scores with 4.1M parameters, 13% of nnU-Net's 31M, so the smaller network loses no measurable accuracy on any of the three organs.
