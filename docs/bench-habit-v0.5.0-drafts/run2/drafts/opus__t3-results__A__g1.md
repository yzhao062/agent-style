Table 3 shows that MedNeXt-Lite improves on nnU-Net for two of the three tasks and shows no detectable difference on the third. For the left ventricle on cardiac MRI, MedNeXt-Lite reaches 89.4% Dice, 3.8 points above nnU-Net (95% CI 2.4 to 5.2; p = 0.002). On lung CT lesions, its margin over nnU-Net is 2.9 points (95% CI 1.1 to 4.7; p = 0.018). Both gains remain significant after Holm-Bonferroni correction across the three tasks. For glioma core on brain MRI, the difference from nnU-Net is 0.4 points, with a 95% CI that spans zero (−0.8 to 1.6; p = 0.48). All three methods score between 87.7% and 88.1% Dice on this task. MedNeXt-Lite's mean Dice also exceeds SwinUNETR's on all three tasks, but we tested differences only against nnU-Net, so these margins remain untested for significance. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these results with 4.1M parameters, about 13% of nnU-Net's 31M.
