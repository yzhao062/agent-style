TrafficCast improves 60-minute forecasting accuracy on the two largest networks, reducing mean absolute error by 11% to 12% relative to GraphWaveNet (Table 7). On Coastal, the 0.02 km/h reduction is small relative to seed variability; on Rural-Net, TrafficCast increases error by 0.19 km/h. These results concentrate the accuracy gains in Metro-A and Metro-B, while showing little benefit on Coastal and worse performance on Rural-Net. Inference time rises from 29 to 41 ms per batch, a 41% increase over GraphWaveNet.
