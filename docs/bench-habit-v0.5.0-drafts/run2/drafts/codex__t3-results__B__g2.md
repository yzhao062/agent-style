MedNeXt-Lite achieved higher Dice scores than both baselines for left ventricular and lung lesion segmentation, while the glioma core difference remained statistically inconclusive (Table 3). Gains over nnU-Net were approximately 3 to 4 percentage points for the cardiac and lung tasks, with both 95% bootstrap confidence intervals excluding zero. For glioma core segmentation, scores clustered closely across all three networks, and the confidence interval for the difference against nnU-Net spanned zero. These results support improved segmentation on the cardiac and lung tasks with a substantially smaller model: MedNeXt-Lite uses 4.1 million parameters versus nnU-Net’s 31 million, a reduction of approximately 87%.
