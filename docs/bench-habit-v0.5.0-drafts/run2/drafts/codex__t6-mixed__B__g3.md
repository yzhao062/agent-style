TabMix achieves its largest accuracy gain on Churn-small, the 2,000-row dataset, exceeding no augmentation by 5.3 percentage points and SMOTE by 3.7 points (Table 6). On Adult and Credit-default, its mean accuracy remains close to both baselines and slightly below SMOTE. On Covertype, TabMix trails no augmentation by 0.2 percentage points. These results show a substantial benefit on Churn-small, with no consistent advantage across the other three datasets.
