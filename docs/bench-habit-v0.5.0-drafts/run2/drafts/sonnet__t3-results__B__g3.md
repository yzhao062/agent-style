MedNeXt-Lite improves on nnU-Net for two of three organs and ties on the third (Table 3). Left-ventricle Dice on cardiac MRI rises by 3.8 points, and lesion Dice on lung CT by 2.9 points. The 95% bootstrap intervals for both differences exclude zero (p = 0.002 and p = 0.018). On brain MRI, the 0.4-point difference in glioma-core Dice lies within noise (interval -0.8 to 1.6, p = 0.48). That interval excludes a loss larger than 0.8 points, so MedNeXt-Lite ties nnU-Net there. SwinUNETR differs from nnU-Net by at most 0.6 points on every task, with heavily overlapping intervals. Against SwinUNETR, MedNeXt-Lite leads by 3.2 points on cardiac MRI and 3.3 on lung CT, but Table 3 reports no test for that comparison. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these results with 4.1M parameters, 7.6 times fewer than the 31M of nnU-Net.
