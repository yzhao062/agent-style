MedNeXt-Lite improved Dice over nnU-Net for left-ventricle and lung-lesion segmentation, while glioma-core segmentation showed no significant difference (Table 3). The cardiac and lung gains were 3.8 and 2.9 percentage points, respectively, with 95% bootstrap confidence intervals excluding zero. For glioma core, the 0.4-point difference remained within statistical uncertainty. Mean Dice also exceeded SwinUNETR across all three tasks, although the brain scores were closely clustered. These cardiac and lung gains accompanied an approximately 87% reduction in parameter count, from 31 million for nnU-Net to 4.1 million for MedNeXt-Lite.
