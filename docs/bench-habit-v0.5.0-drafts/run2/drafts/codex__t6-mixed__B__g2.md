TabMix provides its largest accuracy gain on Churn-small (2,000 rows), improving the mean by 5.3 percentage points over no augmentation and 3.7 points over SMOTE (Table 6). On Adult and Credit-default, gains over no augmentation are small relative to the reported run-to-run variation, and TabMix slightly trails SMOTE. On Covertype, both augmentation methods reduce mean accuracy relative to no augmentation, although TabMix performs better than SMOTE.
