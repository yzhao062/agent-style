Table 6 reports accuracy (%, mean ± standard deviation over 10 runs) for no augmentation, SMOTE, and TabMix on four datasets. TabMix gains most on Churn-small (2,000 rows), where it reaches 81.7%, against 76.4% with no augmentation and 78.0% with SMOTE. The gaps of 5.3 and 3.7 points exceed the standard deviations of 0.9 to 1.1 points more than threefold. On Adult, the three methods land within 0.2 points of each other, inside their standard deviations of 0.2 to 0.3, so they are indistinguishable. Credit-default shows a tie between SMOTE (82.4%) and TabMix (82.3%), and both sit 0.4 to 0.5 points above no augmentation (81.9%), about one standard deviation. Covertype favors no augmentation (92.6%): SMOTE drops 0.5 points to 92.1%, and TabMix drops 0.2 points to 92.4%, a drop within one standard deviation.
