## Conclusion

We presented AdaptRL, which transfers grasping policies from simulation to a real robot by learning residual dynamics online. These learned corrections address differences between simulated and real dynamics. Across 100 real-robot trials per object category, AdaptRL achieves higher grasp success than domain randomization and DAgger in all five categories (Table 5). Success exceeds DAgger by 11 to 24 percentage points, with the largest improvement on reflective objects. Transparent objects remain the weakest category, at 68% success. These results support online residual dynamics learning as an effective approach to grasping policy transfer across the tested categories.
