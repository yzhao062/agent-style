This paper makes two contributions. First, we present FlashKV, a runtime for long-context LLM serving that pages and compresses the key-value (KV) cache, the attention state a model keeps for every earlier token. Second, we evaluate FlashKV against vLLM on Llama-3-70B using one 8xH100 node (Table 2). FlashKV reduces KV-cache memory by 55% at a 4k context and by 63% at 32k and 64k. Its decoding speedup over vLLM is smallest at 4k (1.10x) and rises to 1.48x at 16k and 1.87x at 32k. At 64k, vLLM runs out of memory, while FlashKV still decodes.
