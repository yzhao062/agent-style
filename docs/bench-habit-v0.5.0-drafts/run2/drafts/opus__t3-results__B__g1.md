With 4.1M parameters, about one-eighth of nnU-Net's 31M, MedNeXt-Lite improves Dice over nnU-Net on two of the three tasks (Table 3). The largest gain is on left-ventricle segmentation in cardiac MRI, at 3.8 Dice points (95% CI [2.4, 5.2], p = 0.002), followed by lung lesion segmentation in CT at 2.9 points ([1.1, 4.7], p = 0.018). On brain MRI glioma core, the 0.4-point difference is within noise (CI [-0.8, 1.6], p = 0.48). That interval excludes a loss larger than 0.8 points but gives no evidence of a gain. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net on every task. We did not test SwinUNETR against MedNeXt-Lite, but its point estimates trail by similar margins: 3.2 points on cardiac MRI and 3.3 points on lung CT.
