Table 6 reports test accuracy for no augmentation, SMOTE, and TabMix on four datasets. The clear gain appears on Churn-small (2,000 rows), where TabMix reaches 81.7%, against 76.4% for no augmentation and 78.0% for SMOTE. Those gaps of 5.3 and 3.7 points are several times the run-to-run standard deviations (0.9 to 1.1). The three methods fall within 0.2 points of each other on Adult, comparable to the standard deviations (0.2 to 0.3). On Credit-default, SMOTE (82.4%) and TabMix (82.3%) gain 0.5 and 0.4 points over no augmentation (81.9%), again comparable to the standard deviations (0.4 to 0.5). Covertype favors no augmentation (92.6%): SMOTE loses 0.5 points and TabMix loses 0.2. Because we tested only one small dataset, we cannot say whether the Churn-small gain extends to other low-data settings.
