## Conclusion

AdaptRL transfers grasping policies from simulation to a real robot by learning residual dynamics online. Across the five categories in Table 5, with 100 trials per category, AdaptRL achieves 82.2% mean grasp success. DAgger and domain randomization achieve 63.0% and 46.0%, respectively. AdaptRL improves success in every category, exceeding DAgger by 11 to 24 percentage points.

These results support online residual dynamics learning for the evaluated grasping tasks. Transparent objects remain challenging, with 68% success. Future work should assess whether the gains persist across additional objects, other robots, and longer deployments.
