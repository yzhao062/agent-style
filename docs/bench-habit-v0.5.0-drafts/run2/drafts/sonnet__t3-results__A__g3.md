Table 3 reports Dice scores for the three tasks. MedNeXt-Lite improves on nnU-Net by 3.8 points on cardiac MRI (95% CI [2.4, 5.2], p = 0.002) and by 2.9 points on lung CT (CI [1.1, 4.7], p = 0.018). Both intervals exclude zero. The lung CT p-value exceeds the Bonferroni threshold of 0.017 for three tasks, so that result is the weaker of the two. On brain MRI, the 0.4-point difference (CI [-0.8, 1.6], p = 0.48) is indistinguishable from zero. Its interval includes a loss of 0.8 points, so the data do not establish parity. SwinUNETR differs from nnU-Net by at most 0.6 points on every task, with overlapping intervals on all three, and MedNeXt-Lite exceeds it by 3.2 points on cardiac MRI and 3.3 points on lung CT. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these scores with 4.1M parameters, about 7.6 times fewer than the 31M of nnU-Net.
