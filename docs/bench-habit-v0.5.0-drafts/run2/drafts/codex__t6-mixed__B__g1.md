TabMix improves accuracy most on Churn-small (2,000 rows), exceeding no augmentation by 5.3 percentage points and SMOTE by 3.7 points (Table 6). On Adult and Credit-default, its mean accuracy differs from either baseline by at most 0.4 points, comparable to or smaller than the reported run-to-run standard deviations. On Covertype, TabMix trails no augmentation by 0.2 points and exceeds SMOTE by 0.3 points. These results show a substantial benefit on Churn-small, with limited differences on the other three datasets.
