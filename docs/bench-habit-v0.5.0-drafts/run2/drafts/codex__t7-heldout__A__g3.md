Table 7 shows that TrafficCast reduces 60-minute mean absolute error relative to GraphWaveNet by 11.6% on Metro-A and 10.6% on Metro-B. On Coastal, the 0.02 km/h reduction is small relative to seed variability; on Rural-Net, TrafficCast increases error by 0.19 km/h. Inference takes 41 ms per batch versus 29 ms for GraphWaveNet, a 41% increase. TrafficCast’s accuracy gains are therefore concentrated on the two largest networks evaluated and come with higher inference latency.
