TrafficCast gives its largest gains on the two biggest networks. For 60-minute forecasts, it lowers MAE relative to GraphWaveNet, the strongest baseline, by 0.41 km/h on Metro-A (11.6%) and 0.35 km/h on Metro-B (10.6%), gaps far larger than the seed standard deviations of 0.03 to 0.05 (Table 7). Coastal is a tie: the 0.02 km/h difference from GraphWaveNet lies within the 0.05 to 0.06 seed standard deviations. Rural-Net, the smallest network at 180 sensors, reverses the ordering, with TrafficCast at 3.21 km/h against 3.02 for GraphWaveNet and 3.05 for DCRNN. The advantage shrinks as the sensor count falls from 2,100 to 180 and becomes a loss on the smallest network. TrafficCast also runs slower at inference, taking 41 ms per batch against 29 ms for GraphWaveNet, a 41% increase.
