Table 3 compares each network's Dice score against nnU-Net. MedNeXt-Lite improves left-ventricle segmentation on cardiac MRI by 3.8 points (p = 0.002) and lung lesion segmentation on CT by 2.9 points (p = 0.018). The lung result is the weaker of the two because its p-value would not survive a Bonferroni correction across the three comparisons. On glioma core, MedNeXt-Lite shows no detectable gain: the 0.4-point difference is within noise, with a confidence interval that spans zero (p = 0.48). SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 points of nnU-Net on every task. On cardiac MRI, its interval lies entirely below MedNeXt-Lite's. MedNeXt-Lite reaches these scores with 4.1M parameters, about one-seventh of nnU-Net's 31M.
