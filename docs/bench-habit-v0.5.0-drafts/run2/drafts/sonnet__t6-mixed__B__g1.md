Table 6 reports test accuracy as mean ± std over 10 runs. TabMix helps on one dataset, Churn-small (2,000 rows), where it reaches 81.7% against 76.4% with no augmentation and 78.0% with SMOTE. Those gains of 5.3 and 3.7 points exceed the run-to-run standard deviations of 0.9 to 1.1 points. On Adult and Credit-default, the three methods fall within 0.5 points of one another, about one standard deviation, so we treat them as ties. Both augmentations lower Covertype accuracy. SMOTE loses 0.5 points, more than the standard deviations of 0.1 and 0.2, while TabMix loses 0.2 points, close to the noise level.
