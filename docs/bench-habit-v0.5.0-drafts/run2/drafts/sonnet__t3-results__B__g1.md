MedNeXt-Lite improves on nnU-Net in two of the three tasks (Table 3). On cardiac MRI, it raises left-ventricle Dice by 3.8 points (95% CI [2.4, 5.2], p = 0.002), and on lung CT it raises lesion Dice by 2.9 points ([1.1, 4.7], p = 0.018). The glioma-core difference on brain MRI is 0.4 points, within noise (CI [-0.8, 1.6], p = 0.48), so we find no evidence of a gain there. SwinUNETR stays within 0.6 Dice points of nnU-Net on every task, and its intervals overlap those of nnU-Net throughout. MedNeXt-Lite delivers all three results with 4.1M parameters, about one-eighth of the 31M in nnU-Net.
