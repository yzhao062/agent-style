Table 7 shows that TrafficCast's advantage over the baselines grows with network size. On the two metropolitan networks (1,450 and 2,100 sensors), TrafficCast cuts 60-minute MAE by about 11% relative to GraphWaveNet, the strongest baseline, a margin far beyond seed variation. Coastal (640 sensors) is a tie: the 0.02 km/h difference falls within one standard deviation. Rural-Net (180 sensors) reverses the ranking, with TrafficCast trailing GraphWaveNet by 0.19 km/h and DCRNN by 0.16 km/h, gaps larger than the seed standard deviations. Network size and setting (metropolitan, coastal, rural) vary together across these four networks, so the table cannot separate their effects. TrafficCast is also 41% slower at inference, taking 41 ms per batch against 29 ms for GraphWaveNet. On the metropolitan networks, that extra 12 ms buys an 11% error reduction; for Coastal and Rural-Net, GraphWaveNet matches or beats TrafficCast's accuracy and runs faster.
